Methodology

Project principle

Benfica Analytics studies dimensions of SL Benfica’s performance that are not directly visible in the final score or in the most common statistics.

The metrics are experimental, transparent and reproducible. They may be revised when larger samples, better sources or evidence indicate that an alternative formulation represents the phenomenon more accurately.

Unknown values remain blank. Zero is used only when an event was measured and did not occur.

The metrics are not combined into a single overall match score. Each metric answers a different question and should be interpreted in its own context.

Process and form

xG Difference

xG Difference is the difference between Benfica’s expected goals and the opponent’s expected goals in the same match.

xG Difference = Benfica xG − opponent xG

Interpretation:

  • positive — Benfica generated more xG than the opponent;
  • negative — the opponent generated more xG;
  • close to zero — similar chance production.

It is a process measure and does not guarantee correspondence with the final result.

Reality Gap — Result vs Process

Reality Gap compares the actual result with the result suggested by xG Difference.

The provisional xG result is classified as:

  • xG Difference above +0.25 → suggested win;
  • between −0.25 and +0.25 → suggested draw;
  • below −0.25 → suggested loss.

Reality Gap = actual points − xG-suggested points

Interpretation:

  • 0 — actual result matched the xG-suggested outcome;
  • negative — Benfica earned fewer points than xG suggested;
  • positive — Benfica earned more points than xG suggested.

Reality Gap does not claim what the “correct” result should have been. It measures divergence between result and chance production.

Process Level

Process Level = mean xG Difference over the last 5 matches

Higher values indicate a recent run with a greater advantage in chance production.

Process Volatility

Process Volatility = median |xG Diff(t) − xG Diff(t−1)|

Higher values indicate greater instability in the recent process.

Match dynamics

Threat Timing

Threat Timing measures when Benfica’s attacking threat was concentrated.

Each shot is weighted by a leverage factor based on the minute and the score state immediately before the shot.

Threat Timing = Σ(xG × leverage) / Σ(xG)

Interpretation:

  • above 1 — relatively more threat in higher-leverage moments;
  • close to 1 — approximately neutral distribution;
  • below 1 — relatively more threat in lower-leverage moments.

It does not measure attacking volume; it measures when that volume occurred. For aggregate interpretation, the current version is considered more stable from roughly 15 shots.

Response Threat

Response Threat measures the proportion of attacking threat generated while Benfica were drawing or trailing.

“Response” is defined by the score state at the moment of each shot, not by a fixed time window after conceding.

Tactical Lift — Impact after changes

Observed Change = post-window xG Diff − pre-window xG Diff

The current expected-change relationship from control windows is:

Expected Change = 0.074 − 0.865 × pre_xG_diff

Therefore:

Tactical Lift = Observed Change − Expected Change

Positive values mean the observed change was better than expected; negative values mean it was worse.

Tactical Lift is descriptive, not causal.

LeadLock

LeadLock studies Benfica’s ability to manage situations in which the team leads.

It tracks leads, lost leads, recovered leads, time leading, lead retention and wins after leading. The current version uses a normalised 90-minute analytical clock.

Team structure

Starting XI Familiarity

An XI contains 55 possible pairs. For each current pair, prior joint starts are counted and capped at five.

pair score = min(prior joint starts, 5) / 5

Starting XI Familiarity is the mean across all 55 pairs on a 0–100 scale. Friendlies may contribute to familiarity history.

Pair Coverage

Percentage of the current XI’s 55 pairs that had at least one previous joint start before the match.

Sector Continuity

Measures starter continuity by sector: Defence, Midfield and Attack.

For each current starter, the metric checks the proportion of the previous up to three official matches in which that player also started in the same sector.

Match profiles

GameGrip

GameGrip is the control axis used in the match-profile map.

ChanceCraft

ChanceCraft is the creation-quality axis used alongside GameGrip.

The map is descriptive: it compares how Benfica controlled and created rather than producing one overall match score.

Outfield players

Player metrics use the 2025/26 Benfica baseline and:

Score = 50 + 50 × tanh(z / 3.33)

A score around 50 represents the baseline reference.

Threat

  • xG/90 — 40%
  • shots/90 — 20%
  • shots on target/90 — 15%
  • xGOT/90 — 15%
  • goals/90 — 10%

Chance Creation

  • key passes/90 — 30%
  • big chances created/90 — 25%
  • xA/90 — 20%
  • assists/90 — 15%
  • accurate crosses/90 — 10%

Progression

  • accurate final-third passes/90 — 45%
  • final-third entries/90 — 35%
  • penalty-area entries/90 — 20%

Ball Security

Uses position-specific baselines for Defence (D), Midfield (M) and Forward (F).

  • pass accuracy — 35%
  • turnovers/90, inverse — 30%
  • dribble efficiency — 20%
  • duel efficiency — 15%

Defensive Contribution

  • recoveries — 20%
  • interceptions — 20%
  • won tackles — 20%
  • won duels — 15%
  • blocks — 10%
  • clearances — 10%
  • challenges lost — −5%

Threat Execution — Finishing quality

Threat Execution = (xGOT − xG) / shots

For match ranking, at least 2 shots are required. For season ranking, at least 10 shots are required.

Sample reliability

  • Small sample — fewer than 3 matches with 30+ minutes and fewer than 180 total minutes;
  • Developing — at least 3 matches with 30+ minutes or at least 180 total minutes;
  • Reliable — at least 8 matches with 30+ minutes and at least 300 total minutes.

Goalkeepers

BuildKeeper

BuildKeeper is a construction-oriented goalkeeper metric, not an overall goalkeeper rating.

The 2025/26 historical reference currently covers Liga matches only.

Context

Rest Days

Days since the previous Benfica match.

Games in the last 14 days

Number of Benfica matches in the previous 14-day window.

These remain contextual indicators; no separate Schedule Load score is used.

Experimental, deferred and archived

Starter Association — experimental

Explores the association between player starts and team xG Difference. It is not treated as causal.

Structural Dependency — deferred

Deferred until stronger opponent/context adjustment and a larger sample are available.

New Signing Integration — deferred

Deferred because the current sample is still insufficient.

Impact Above Expectation — archived

Archived because the attempted individual attribution relied too heavily on collective team outcomes.

Sources

The project combines multiple sources where appropriate, including SofaScore, FotMob, Flashscore, FBref, football-data.org, OpenLigaDB, public datasets and manually exported OddAlerts data.

Methodological rules

  • No overall match score.
  • Unknown values remain blank rather than being forced to zero.
  • Metrics are descriptive unless explicitly stated otherwise.
  • Player and team outputs remain technically separate.
  • Conditional metrics are omitted when events or coverage are absent.
  • Definitions, thresholds and formulas may be revised when better evidence becomes available.
